## Problems found and fixed for run 6 (runtime `a1fb9258…`, loader `4ab49ab7…`, both pushed and verified over FTP)

- **8 engine scripts recompiled every launch:**
  - which: `common/_layout/*.rpym` and `common/_developer/*.rpym`;
  - effect: Ren'Py tried to write `.rpymc` to read-only RomFS, wasting start-up time and
    logging errors;
  - fix: staging now copies the SDK's compiled files from sub-folders as well (74 verified).
    18 legacy-layout files whose digests do not match are skipped; Ren'Py compiles those
    only if a game uses them.
- **`~` unset:**
  - effect: Ren'Py made backups and its save-token folder under a literal `~` folder
    (`sdmc:/switch/renpy-loader/runtimes/~/…`);
  - fix: `HOME=sdmc:/switch/renpy-loader/home`, and developer backups are off
    (`RENPY_DISABLE_BACKUPS`), which also saves about 1.3 s per launch.
- **Heat:**
  - the loader redrew its CPU-rendered text screen 60 times a second while idle; it now
    redraws on input, on the live test screen, or once a second;
  - the bridge polled every 16 ms even when idle; it now polls every 33 ms after 2 s
    without input.

  Desktop scenario still 24/24.
- Steam init fails (`_ctypes` missing): harmless, logged by Ren'Py.
